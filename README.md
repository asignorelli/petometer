# Petometer

Petometer is an app that combines a step counter with a virtual pet! The more steps you take each day equates to more coins that you can then spend on your pet!

# Features
The app has three tabs:


-one tab for a step counter which displays the amount of steps you have taken so far,
your step goal, and then your active streak of how many days you reached your step
goal.

-one tab for the pet in its living area. Here you can view your pet, its mood and health,
feed it, change its outfit, and decorate its living spac

-one for the marketplace. This is where you purchase the food, clothes, and furniture
that you want for your pet.

**Step counter features**:

The step counter keeps track of how many steps the user takes each day and calculates the resulting coins earned. These are accumulated in the coin bank which can be collected as the user wishes. 
There are also be daily goals the user can complete to gain bonus coins. This will
allow you to keep your pet happy/fed on busy days you can’t walk, or for people who
struggle to get steps each day. These will be goals such as:
- getting enough water
- enough sleep
- going outside
The user can also set a step goal each day (such as 10,000) and if they reach it, they
will be rewarded with bonus coins.

**Pet features**:

A pet’s wellbeing consists of its health and mood.
- Health is impacted by how much you feed your pet. You must spend coins on
food often or else the pet’s health will dip too low and it will die, requiring you
to spawn a new one. The food you feed your pet will be based on real life
healthy meals to inspire healthy eating for the user.

**Marketplace features:**

- Another way to spend coins is for customization (clothing/furniture). The
clothing will be different hats/wigs you can buy, and then the furniture will be
for the living area. Each one provides a permanent boost to the pet’s mood. A
happy pet’s health will decrease slower

This app competes with other step tracking apps, but it is unique in that it is also tied
to a unique pet that holds you accountable. It helps promote health, provide
motivation to users, and makes walking more fun!
